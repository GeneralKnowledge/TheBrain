# Seed notes: one per line. Lines ending with '?' become open questions.
# These are deliberately mundane and mixed so that association has material.
The town's clock tower runs four minutes slow every winter.
Moss grows thicker on the north side of the old stone wall.
Bread dough rises more slowly in the cold pantry.
The river froze completely only twice in the last fifty years.
Migrating geese arrived two weeks earlier this year than last year.
The library's oldest map shows a road that no longer exists.
Copper roofs turn green over several decades.
Why does the clock tower lose time only in winter?
What made the geese arrive earlier this year?
Where did the road on the old map lead?
